Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026

Countdown Timers does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data Collection

Countdown Timers does not collect any personal information, usage data, analytics, or diagnostic data. The app does not include third-party trackers, advertising networks, or analytics software of any kind.

Data Storage

All timers, event names, dates, and preferences you create are stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party. We do not operate any servers that receive or process your data.

Data Sharing

Because no data is collected, no data is shared with third parties, advertisers, or other users. Countdown Timers does not integrate with any third-party services that would receive your information.

Children's Privacy

Countdown Timers does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect data from any user.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that involves data collection — this page will be updated and the "Effective date" above will reflect the change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@theswensons.org.
